# Telegram 101 for FM-1

Build 857 · Player guide

Telegram 101 puts a 101 lead, Dillo acid bass and an 808 drum machine on the M-VAVE FM-1.

[Install Telegram 101 for FM-1](https://thebigbeat.app/fm1/telegram-101/install/) · [Download Build 857](https://github.com/TexasRADIO/telegram-101-fm1-firmware/releases/tag/build-857) · [Support](https://github.com/TexasRADIO/telegram-101-fm1-firmware/issues)

## Install by USB

Back up your work. Connect one powered instrument directly with a USB data cable. Close other apps using its MIDI port. In desktop Chrome or Edge, open the installer, click Install Telegram 101 and allow MIDI access. Keep the cable connected until the page confirms completion. The downloadable ZIP includes the same installer for offline use.

## Play your first sound

Play a white key. Turn PRESETS to hear the next sound. On Home, turn ALGORITHM to move between banks. On the first knob page, knobs 1–4 change cutoff, resonance, envelope amount and decay. SELECT moves to the next knob page.

## Shape a sound

Hold a note and press EDIT. Each further press moves through Source Mix, Filter, Envelope and Modulator. Turn the four knobs under the screen to adjust the controls shown. ENV takes you straight to the envelope. HOME takes you back.

## Record a knob for one bar

On a Telegram playing page, turn a sound knob until its card reads REC: RECORD A BAR. Press REC while the card is visible, then move that knob. Your first movement starts the recording. After one bar it loops. Turning the same knob shifts the gesture around its new setting. To clear the loop, bring that knob’s card back and press REC. One gesture plays at a time. It follows pattern changes and clears when you load another sound or power off.

## Play the Band

From Telegram Home, press HOME again for Band. Press SAVE to open Songs and use a white key to pick a part. Press PLAY. Turn SELECT clockwise to cue the next section on the next bar, or anticlockwise for the previous section. Hold GLO and press SCL to switch the keys between Telegram and Dillo.

## Library and backups

SAVE on Telegram Home opens the sound library. Coming from BB1, Telegram asks for confirmation before the first library save replaces the BB1 factory sound bank. You can audition sounds and play songs before making that decision.

## Interrupted install and return

If the transfer was interrupted and the FM-1 is still in its MIDI updater, reconnect it and use Resume install. A physical return from Build 857 to M-VAVE factory V15 remains unverified. If you depend on that return, keep your current firmware.
